Meet PAXELUNE LIFE

YOUR LIFE. YOUR SYSTEM.

PAXELUNE LIFE is a personal life management workspace uniting Tasks, Calendar Planner, Life Goals, Habits, Reminders, Journal, Notes, Personal Finance, Wellness Tracking, and Focus Sessions.

Your data is stored offline-first in private Internal Storage, with optional Google Sign-In and cloud backup synchronization powered by Google Firebase Cloud Firestore.
