PAXELUNE LIFE · VERSION 26.1.0

Privacy Policy

Effective for PAXELUNE LIFE v26.1.0 · Google Play Data Safety. This page describes the data practices stated in the application's supplied Privacy screen.

1. Data You Create & Optional Google Sign-In

PAXELUNE LIFE stores personal records that you actively create: Tasks, Planner Events, Life Goals, Habits, Reminders, Journal entries, Notes (including optional photo attachments), Finance accounts/transactions/budgets/savings goals, Wellness & Medical Checkup notes, and Focus sessions.

Full offline use requires no account. If you choose Sign in with Google (via Firebase Authentication & Android Credential Manager), we process your Google UID, display name, email address, and profile photo URL solely for authentication and per-user data isolation.

Your optional App Lock PIN is hashed locally with salted PBKDF2 and is never uploaded to the cloud.

2. Internal Storage, Firebase Cloud Firestore & Security

By default, all data is stored offline-first in the app's private Internal Storage (isolated Room database & DataStore with allowBackup=false).

When you sign in with Google, data synchronizes over encrypted HTTPS/TLS to Google Firebase Cloud Firestore protected by strict owner-only Firestore Security Rules (only your authenticated UID can read, write, or delete your documents).

3. Device Permissions (Google Play Least-Privilege)

Camera (CAMERA)

Optional, requested in-context only when you choose Camera to attach a photo to a task or note.

Photo Selection

Uses the zero-permission Android Photo Picker without broad storage permissions (never requests READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE).

Notifications, Vibration & Boot

POST_NOTIFICATIONS, VIBRATE, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: optional, used strictly to deliver and restore your scheduled reminders.

Network

INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE are used for optional Google Sign-In and Firebase Cloud Firestore sync.

Not Accessed

PAXELUNE LIFE does not access GPS location, Health Connect, contacts, SMS, call logs, or background microphone.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Protection

PAXELUNE LIFE uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore) as user-authorized authentication and cloud backup infrastructure. We never sell or rent your personal records to any third party.

If additional services are introduced in future updates to support app maintenance, this policy and Data Safety disclosures will be updated transparently in accordance with Google Play policies.

5. Retention, Portability & Account/Data Deletion

Local data remains on your device while installed; synced cloud records remain in Firebase Cloud Firestore until you delete them.

You can export all personal data anytime (JSON/PDF/CSV) or permanently delete both local device records and all synced Firebase Cloud Firestore records directly in-app using the "Delete Account & Data" button in the app's privacy settings.

For privacy or deletion inquiries, contact support.paxelune@gmail.com.
